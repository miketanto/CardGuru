## Question

> cards that exile the top of my library and let me play those cards, but only for a limited time

**What makes it tricky:** Exile-then-permission-then-time-limit is a three-part structure with no keyword; Bomat Courier exiles cards and later returns them to hand, matching most of the words and none of the structure.

**Cards already accepted as satisfying it** (for a consistent reading, not as a pattern to match): Light Up the Stage, Outpost Siege, Chandra, Torch of Defiance

**Cards already rejected**: Bomat Courier

## Answer format

Write JSON to `verdicts/impulse-exile-then-cast.json`: an object mapping each card name to `"present"`, `"absent"` or `"unclear"`.

- `present` — it clearly satisfies the question.
- `absent`  — it clearly does not.
- `unclear` — the question wording genuinely does not decide it. Use this freely; a forced call is worse than no call.

Every one of the cards listed must appear exactly once.
