## Question

> abilities I can activate as many times as I have mana for - nothing in the cost that limits it

**What makes it tricky:** This is a constraint over what the cost does NOT contain (no tap symbol, no sacrifice, no counter removal, no once-each-turn rider) - a negative structural query rather than a positive pattern match.

**Author's notes:** Flagged suspect because negation over cost components is the kind of thing a structural search may simply not express. The answer set would also be enormous, so it may be ill-posed on recall grounds too.

**Cards already rejected**: Icy Manipulator, Hangarback Walker
