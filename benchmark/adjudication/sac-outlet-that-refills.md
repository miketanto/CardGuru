# Adjudication: `sac-outlet-that-refills`

Decide, for each card below, whether it satisfies the QUESTION.
Judge the card on its own text. There is no query here and you do not know which search returned it — that is deliberate.

## Question

> free sac outlets that also replace the creature I sacrificed

**What makes it tricky:** 'Free' (no mana in the cost), 'sac outlet' (sacrifice-a-creature as a cost you choose), and 'replaces' (draws or makes a body) are three constraints, one of which is player jargon that appears on no card.

**Author's notes:** Left expect_present empty on purpose - I could not name one I was fully certain of that satisfies all three constraints. The absent cards are free sac outlets that do not replace the body.

**Cards already rejected**: Ashnod's Altar, Viscera Seer

## Answer format

Write JSON to `verdicts/sac-outlet-that-refills.json`: an object mapping each card name to `"present"`, `"absent"` or `"unclear"`.

- `present` — it clearly satisfies the question.
- `absent`  — it clearly does not.
- `unclear` — the question wording genuinely does not decide it. Use this freely; a forced call is worse than no call.

Every one of the cards listed must appear exactly once.
